Excitation, texture and colour

Transient and texture are separately enveloped. Low-rate and bit-depth controls describe explicit early-digital replay colour; 48 kHz / 24-bit is clean.

Deterministic velocity response

Ghost, normal and accent bands independently drive model excitation and output gain. Realised values are repeatable and reported by the renderer.
